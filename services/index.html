Design-build sequence

Five gates from brief to handover.

Each gate ends with a clear decision package so scope, cost and design intent remain aligned.

Discover

Business brief, site constraints, operational requirements, programme and project governance.

Define

Spatial strategy, test fit, concept direction and early cost alignment.

Detail

Technical documentation, material approvals, coordinated scope and procurement package.

Deliver

Site mobilisation, programme control, installation, quality review and client reporting.

Close

Snagging, documentation, handover and post-occupancy review.

Service FAQs
Can Orivane work with our existing architect or contractor?

Yes. The fictional service model can be scoped for strategy, interior design, technical coordination or delivery support only, with responsibilities agreed at project start.

Do you handle authority submissions?

Where statutory submissions or licensed professionals are required, the project plan would include the appropriate registered consultants and building-management requirements. This portfolio site does not claim regulatory credentials.

Can projects be phased around live operations?

Yes. Refresh and retrofit scopes can be sequenced by zone, after-hours window or weekend access where the site and landlord conditions allow it.
