02 · AboutDesign-build, without the disconnect

Commercial design with operational discipline.

Orivane is a fictional Selangor-based SME concept built around one idea: the people who define the design intent should remain accountable for how it reaches site.

Our perspective

A space should earn its place on the balance sheet.

Commercial interiors absorb serious capital. Our process treats design decisions as business decisions—balancing experience, brand, occupancy, durability, maintenance and delivery risk.

That means fewer speculative gestures, earlier technical thinking and more time spent understanding what needs to work after opening day.

Our story

Built from the gap between design and delivery.

2018

Started as a small spatial design studio

The fictional founding team focused on compact offices, boutique retail and project briefing for growth-stage businesses in Klang Valley.

2020

Integrated delivery capability

Client demand shifted toward fewer hand-offs, so Orivane added procurement, technical coordination and site delivery under one structure.

2023

Expanded beyond Klang Valley

Partner networks and a repeatable delivery playbook enabled coordinated projects in Penang, Johor, Sabah and Sarawak.

Today

Focused on established SMEs and commercial teams

The studio is positioned for projects where design quality, business continuity and delivery accountability matter equally.
