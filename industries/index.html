How we adapt

Same discipline. Different priorities.

Workplace: change management matters

We map team behaviours, privacy needs, leadership expectations and workplace policies before layout decisions harden.

Retail: circulation is commercial

Planning is shaped by visibility, dwell, product hierarchy, staff operations and the path to transaction.

Hospitality: atmosphere must survive operations

Lighting, acoustics and material tactility are balanced against cleaning, service routes and repeatable maintenance.

Rollouts: consistency needs a system

Prototype details, cost logic and approved alternatives are documented so brand intent survives site variation.
