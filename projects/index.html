Case study · Aurora Capital

Turning privacy into a planning principle.

Challenge

The fictional brief required a premium client experience without exposing sensitive internal workflows or over-isolating teams.

Response

Client-facing suites occupy the arrival edge, while a controlled internal spine connects focus rooms, team neighbourhoods and leadership areas. Material transitions reinforce the change from public to private without relying on signage.

Commercial logic

The plan concentrates fixed construction where privacy demands it, preserving more adaptable open areas for future team growth.
